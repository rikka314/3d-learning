# Storyboard Prompts: Vision Banana

共同视觉设定：连贯的五幕科学教育漫画；主角是一台友善、香蕉形轮廓的黄色“画家机器人”，佩戴圆形相机镜头与蓝色围巾；同一间明亮的视觉实验室、同一角色设计、同一平涂加轻微水彩质感；无品牌 logo、无水印、尽量无文字。所有画面只把报告中已核验的机制转成视觉隐喻。

## S1 — 会画但不会按答题卡作答

Use case: illustration-story  
Asset type: connected paper-reading storyboard panel 1 of 5  
Primary request: The recurring yellow banana-shaped painter robot observes one photograph and freely paints beautiful but inconsistent segmentation-like, depth-like, and normal-like images; a benchmark examiner cannot score them because the colors and formats drift.  
Composition/framing: landscape panel, robot and paintings central, confused measuring tools at the side.  
Constraints: no readable text; no equations; no logos; no watermark; visually imply latent understanding but unmeasurable output, not failure of vision.

Linked claims: `C4.1`, `C6.1`.

## S2 — 教会可逆 RGB 答题协议

Use case: illustration-story  
Asset type: connected paper-reading storyboard panel 2 of 5  
Primary request: The same robot learns three precise visual answer cards: prompt-specified colored masks, a winding RGB-cube depth rail that maps near and far objects to colors, and a three-channel surface-normal compass; only a small handful of lesson cards is mixed into a large stack of its original art lessons.  
Composition/framing: landscape classroom-lab scene, clear three-part protocol, small lesson stack versus large art stack.  
Constraints: no readable text; no formulas; no logos; no watermark; make the small-ratio mixture visually unambiguous.

Linked claims: `C2.1`, `C11.2`, `C14.2`.

## S3 — 一个模型、多个任务

Use case: illustration-story  
Asset type: connected paper-reading storyboard panel 3 of 5  
Primary request: The same robot receives one street photograph and one interchangeable prompt card, then produces five clean, decodable RGB answer cards representing semantic segmentation, instance segmentation, referring segmentation, metric depth, and surface normals; deterministic decoder tools convert them into masks, meters, and arrows.  
Composition/framing: landscape hub-and-spoke composition, one robot in the center, five outputs, compact decoder instruments.  
Constraints: no readable text; no logos; no watermark; outputs must look distinct and technically plausible.

Linked claims: `C7.1`, `C9.1`, `C13.1`.

## S4 — 强结果与边界同时出现

Use case: illustration-story  
Asset type: connected paper-reading storyboard panel 4 of 5  
Primary request: The same robot completes a multi-lane vision benchmark course beside specialist machines for segmentation, depth, and normals; it performs strongly across most lanes while visibly stumbling slightly at one instance-segmentation hurdle, one outdoor-normal hurdle, and an editing balance scale near fifty-fifty.  
Composition/framing: landscape benchmark arena, celebratory but measured tone, strengths and weak spots equally visible.  
Constraints: no readable text or invented numbers; no logos; no watermark; do not portray universal victory.

Linked claims: `C17.1`, `C17.2`, `C17.3`, `C17.4`.

## S5 — 下一篇论文的问题

Use case: illustration-story  
Asset type: connected paper-reading storyboard panel 5 of 5  
Primary request: The same robot and a scientist inspect two transparent layers inside its head labeled only through icons: a huge pretraining memory layer and a small instruction lesson layer; their contributions are entangled. Around them are four future experiments represented by icons: controlled comparison scales, uncertainty around an RGB rail, coherent video frames, and a lightweight distilled robot.  
Composition/framing: landscape research-roadmap finale, magnifying glass and branching experiment paths.  
Constraints: no readable text; no logos; no watermark; emphasize the unresolved unlocked-versus-relearned question.

Linked claims: `C18.2`, `C23.2`, `C23.3`, `C23.4`.
