## Problem

Video/multi-view diffusion learns consistency implicitly and often produces 3D-inconsistent content; explicit 3D models can be hard to scale.

## Core idea

Represent per-pixel global 3D coordinates as XYZ images and train a diffusion transformer jointly over RGB and XYZ frames, using inpainting for several 3D tasks.

## Claimed contributions

- A single model that predicts “6D videos” (RGB + XYZ).
- Single-image-to-3D, multi-view stereo and camera-controlled video through one inference interface.
- Competitive results on multi-view and depth benchmarks.

## Method

Train DiT on mixed RGB/XYZ sequences. At inference, provide any observed RGB/XYZ channels, mask the missing channels, and inpaint them under camera-trajectory conditions.

## Experimental setup
- Datasets/simulators/robots: RealEstate10K, ScanNet, MVImgNet, CO3D and Habitat for training; NYU-v2, BONN and ScanNet++ depth tests plus multi-view evaluations.
- Baselines: Multi-view/video diffusion systems, depth methods and geometric reconstruction baselines.
- Metrics: Single-image-to-3D and depth benchmark metrics; exact table metric names/values should be checked in the PDF.
- Ablations: Joint RGB+XYZ prediction and conditioning/inpainting strategy.
- Sim-only, real-robot, or mixed: Static real/synthetic scene data; no robot experiment.

## Main evidence
- What the paper directly supports: Joint RGB-XYZ diffusion yields competitive 3D-consistent generation and reported depth/multi-view gains [Paper Abstract, §§4–5].
- What remains unsupported or weak: The model is trained only on static datasets.

## Limitations and failure cases

The authors explicitly restrict the current system to static scenes; dynamic 4D learning, resolution and stronger camera/geometry coverage remain future work [Paper Limitations].

## What is reusable

XYZ supervision is a simple, explicit channel for coupling generated appearance with geometry and may be adapted to anatomy coordinate maps.

## What is questionable

Coordinate-map consistency is not a mesh/topology guarantee and does not establish physical dynamics.

## Relation to our project

Useful as an explicit geometry condition for static medical scene/view synthesis; pair with D4RT/4D-LRM for temporal anatomy.

## Citation notes
- Safe claims this paper can support: RGB+XYZ joint modeling, task unification, data mix and static-scene limitation (direct).
- Claims this paper should not be used to support: Dynamic 4D or clinically correct reconstruction (unsupported).
