## Problem

Most 3D benchmarks score rendered appearance, not whether an LLM-generated parametric program is executable, dimensionally correct, topologically valid and assembly-consistent.

## Core idea

Evaluate Text-to-3D, Image-to-3D and Assembly-3D through executable programs, using four buckets: Geo, Topo, Judge and Part.

## Claimed contributions

- P3D-Dataset with 400 text cases, 400 image cases and 203 assemblies.
- A unified protocol across four code formats (JSON, OpenSCAD, Blender and Three.js).
- Evaluation of frontier MLLMs, text-only LLMs and domain-specific systems.

## Method

Execute generated programs, render the result, then score validity, geometric fidelity, topology, MLLM judgment and part structure against specifications/annotations.

## Experimental setup
- Datasets/simulators/robots: Text2CAD and Fusion 360-derived CAD sources; executable CAD/program outputs.
- Baselines: Frontier MLLMs and CAD-specialized models listed by the paper.
- Metrics: Executability plus Geo, Topo, Judge and Part scores; scores are rescaled to 0–100.
- Ablations: Format/task and bucket analyses are reported.
- Sim-only, real-robot, or mixed: Programmatic/CAD evaluation; no robot experiment.

## Main evidence
- What the paper directly supports: Current models can produce plausible-looking but dimensionally inaccurate or structurally incomplete parametric outputs [Paper Abstract, §3, Conclusion].
- What remains unsupported or weak: The benchmark's CAD domain and fixed cases may not represent open-world anatomical structures.

## Limitations and failure cases

Dataset size is modest relative to open-world 3D; CAD syntax and renderer choices affect validity; MLLM judges can add evaluator bias.

## What is reusable

The executable-artifact protocol is a strong template for evaluating anatomical procedural models: execute, inspect topology, compare measurements and score parts separately.

## What is questionable

Parametric CAD correctness does not imply physical, biological or clinical correctness.

## Relation to our project

Useful if the project moves toward procedural organ models, surgical planning scenes or editable anatomy programs; it complements voxel/mesh metrics.

## Citation notes
- Safe claims this paper can support: Task definitions, dataset counts and four evaluation buckets (direct).
- Claims this paper should not be used to support: General MLLM reasoning ability outside the benchmark or medical validity (unsupported).
